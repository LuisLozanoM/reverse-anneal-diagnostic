# Editorial Portal Text

## Short Significance Statement

Programmable quantum annealers can relax embedded subsystems into calibrated conditional thermal marginals under controlled bath size and coupling, while disorder and atypical bath preparations arrest that relaxation.

## 30-Word Summary

Two D-Wave annealers show controllable subsystem relaxation: larger or stronger environments erase memory, disorder arrests it, and relaxed readout follows calibrated conditional Boltzmann marginals.

## 100-150 Word Editorial Summary

This manuscript reports a controlled subsystem-environment experiment on programmable quantum annealers. A small subsystem is embedded in an on-chip environment during reverse annealing, with independent control over bath size, subsystem-environment coupling, disorder strength, environment preparation, pause point, graph geometry and QPU generation. The experiments show a tunable crossover from memory retention to initial-state-independent relaxation as the environment grows or the boundary coupling increases. Disorder and atypical environment preparations arrest the crossover. When relaxation occurs, the final subsystem readout follows a calibrated conditional Boltzmann marginal across geometries, couplings, disorder levels and two hardware generations. A mixed-frustration regime further shows that the relaxation rate is not reproduced by matched-temperature single-spin-flip Glauber dynamics, consistent with a transverse-field-assisted pathway during reverse annealing.

## If Asked Why Nature Physics
